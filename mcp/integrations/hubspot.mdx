The Cominty app for HubSpot lets your Cominty agents work with your HubSpot
CRM in plain language. Agents can:

- Search and read contacts, companies, deals, tickets, and activities.
- Summarize an account before a meeting, or list the open deals of a pipeline.
- Create or update records, notes, and tasks, **only after you confirm** the
  proposed change.

Cominty uses the remote HubSpot MCP server, hosted by HubSpot at
`https://mcp.hubspot.com`. Every action respects your existing HubSpot user
permissions: the agent only sees and changes what your HubSpot user can see
and change.

## Before you start

- You have a Cominty account on [platform.cominty.ai](https://platform.cominty.ai).
  To share the connection with your whole workspace, you need to be a Cominty
  admin.
- You have a HubSpot account, and a HubSpot user allowed to install apps (a
  **Super Admin**, or a user with the **App Marketplace** permission).
- There is no OAuth client to create: Cominty provides the app, named
  **Cominty for Hubspot** in HubSpot. The connection uses OAuth 2.1 with PKCE.

<Tip>
  Connect with a HubSpot user whose permissions match what you want the agents
  to reach. The agent never gets more access than that user.
</Tip>

## Install the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the integration">
    On [platform.cominty.ai](https://platform.cominty.ai), open **Integrations**
    in the left menu. Search for `HubSpot` and click the **HubSpot Official**
    card.

    <Frame caption="Search for HubSpot and choose HubSpot Official">
      ![The Cominty Integrations page, filtered on HubSpot, with the HubSpot Official card](/images/mcp/hubspot-integrations.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can use it">
    The **HubSpot Official** dialog opens. Under **Availability**, pick
    **Organization** to share the connection with everyone in your workspace
    (admins manage it), or **Personal** to keep it private to you.

    Under **Configuration**, the HubSpot authentication (`HUBSPOTOFFICIALAUTH`)
    is already set: there is nothing to fill in. You can fill in **Agent
    configuration** now or later. See [Configure the app](#configure-the-app).

    <Frame caption="The HubSpot Official dialog: Availability and Configuration">
      ![The HubSpot Official integration dialog in Cominty, with Personal selected and the Connect button](/images/mcp/hubspot-dialog.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. A HubSpot page opens: "Connect Cominty for Hubspot to
    HubSpot". Sign in to HubSpot if asked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your HubSpot account">
    Select the HubSpot account to connect, then click **Select account**.

    <Frame caption="Selecting the HubSpot account to connect">
      ![The HubSpot account selection screen for Cominty for Hubspot](/images/mcp/hubspot-choose-account.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and approve the requested access">
    HubSpot lists what Cominty for Hubspot can access in your account:

    - **Currently granted**: the required scopes. Expand the section to see the
      full list (CRM records, activities, properties, owners, pipelines,
      conversations, marketing data).
    - **Optional**: extra scopes you can choose to grant, such as creating and
      editing custom objects, their properties and pipelines. Some optional
      scopes need a higher HubSpot subscription (for example Enterprise).
      HubSpot shows a lock and a tooltip when they are not available.

    Review the list, then click **Connect app**.

    <Frame caption="The optional scopes, with a lock on those that need a higher subscription">
      ![The HubSpot screen listing the optional scopes requested by Cominty for Hubspot, with a tooltip on a locked one](/images/mcp/hubspot-scopes.png)
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="The Currently granted section expanded, and the Connect app button">
      ![The HubSpot screen with the Currently granted scopes expanded and the Connect app button](/images/mcp/hubspot-connect-app.png)
    </Frame>

    <Note>
      Until the app is verified by HubSpot, HubSpot shows an "unverified app"
      warning and asks you to type a confirmation sentence before you click
      **Connect**. This step disappears once the app is listed on the HubSpot
      Marketplace.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the status">
    You come back to the Integrations page of Cominty. Open the
    **HubSpot Official** card again: the dialog shows a **Connected** badge.
    Click **Test connection** to check it.

    <Frame caption="HubSpot connected in Cominty">
      ![The HubSpot Official dialog with the Connected badge, the Enabled toggle, and the Test connection button](/images/mcp/hubspot-connected.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Open a new conversation and ask: "Who am I in HubSpot, and which objects
    can I access?"
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  If the connection fails after you approve the access, or if you need help,
  reach out to [hello@cominty.ai](mailto:hello@cominty.ai).
</Info>

## Configure the app

HubSpot questions often need context the agent cannot guess: your pipelines,
your key properties, your conventions. Put them in the integration's
**Agent configuration**, once, instead of typing them in every message.

The two fields do different jobs:

| Field | Read by | Use it for |
| ----- | ------- | ---------- |
| **When to use** | The main agent, to decide when to hand a task to HubSpot | The situations HubSpot covers: clients, deals, contacts, tickets |
| **How to use it** | The HubSpot sub-agent that does the work | Pipelines, key properties, defaults, rules |

Example for **How to use it**:

```text
"Clients" are companies with the lifecycle stage Customer.
The main sales pipeline is "Sales Pipeline". Renewals are in "Renewals".
The renewal date is in the company property renewal_date.
Never create or update a record without showing the change and getting confirmation.
Answer in the user's language.
```

For the full model (what each field does, how the main agent and the sub-agent
share the work, and how to write good instructions), see
[Connector instructions](/mcp/connector-instructions).

## Use the app

There is no background sync. The agent acts only when a user asks it to, in a
Cominty conversation. To point a message at HubSpot, type `@hub` and pick
**HubSpot Official** from the menu, then write your request.

<Frame caption="Picking HubSpot Official from the chat with @hub">
  ![The Cominty chat input with @hub typed and HubSpot Official suggested in the Integrations menu](/images/mcp/hubspot-use-in-chat.png)
</Frame>

**Read actions** run directly. Example prompts:

- "Summarize the company Acme: contacts, open deals, and last activities."
- "List the deals in the stage Negotiation that close this quarter."
- "Find the open tickets for the company Acme."

**Write actions** always need your confirmation. The agent shows the proposed
change, and nothing is written until you confirm:

- "Add a note to the deal Acme Renewal with the summary of today's meeting."
- "Create a follow-up task for next Monday on the contact Jane Doe."

### What agents can do

Available tools depend on your HubSpot subscription, your user permissions,
and your account configuration. Main tools:

| Tool | What it does |
| ---- | ------------ |
| `get_user_details` | Read the connected user, the account, and the objects you can read or write |
| `search_crm_objects` | Search and filter CRM records |
| `get_crm_objects` | Fetch records by ID |
| `query_crm_data` | Query CRM data with filters, aggregation, and sorting |
| `search_properties` / `get_properties` | Find and read property definitions |
| `search_owners` | Find record owners |
| `manage_crm_objects` | Create or update records and activities, after confirmation |

## Limits

- A search returns 200 results per page at most. A fetch by ID takes 100 IDs
  at most.
- `query_crm_data` does not support joins, subqueries, or association
  traversal.
- Search is based on the HubSpot CRM search API, without semantic search.
- When HubSpot adds tools, new scopes may be needed: reconnect the app to grant
  them.

## Manage the connection

Open **Integrations** → **HubSpot Official** at any time to:

- **Pause it**: turn off **Enabled** ("Allow the agent to use this
  integration"). The connection is kept, but agents stop using HubSpot until
  you turn it back on.
- **Share it**: click **Move to organization** to make a personal connection
  available to your whole workspace.
- **Check it**: click **Test connection**.

From your code, you can turn the connection off for one message, or limit a
thread to some connections. See
[Capabilities](/guides/capabilities) and the
[use case on connections](/guides/capabilities-use-cases#recipe-3-keep-some-connections-or-none).

## Disconnect the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the integration">
    On [platform.cominty.ai](https://platform.cominty.ai), open **Integrations**
    and choose **HubSpot Official**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disconnect">
    Click **Disconnect**. The HubSpot access token is revoked, and the HubSpot
    MCP server is no longer reachable from Cominty.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **What happens to your data:**

  - Cominty agents can no longer read or write your HubSpot data.
  - Nothing is deleted in HubSpot. Records, notes, and tasks created through
    Cominty stay in your account.
  - Cominty does not sync or copy your CRM: agents query HubSpot live. HubSpot
    data quoted in past conversations stays in your Cominty history until you
    delete those conversations.
  - To request deletion of your data, contact
    [privacy@cominty.ai](mailto:privacy@cominty.ai). See our
    [Privacy Policy](https://app.cominty.ai/policies/privacy-policy).
</Warning>

## Uninstall the app

Cominty for Hubspot is not installed inside your HubSpot account: it is an
OAuth connection between Cominty and HubSpot. **Disconnecting it in Cominty, as
described above, is enough to remove all access.** There is nothing else to
uninstall.

If you prefer to revoke the access from HubSpot, follow the HubSpot article
[Uninstall an app](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/integrations/connect-apps-to-hubspot#uninstall-an-app).
The effect on your data is the same as disconnecting.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause |
| ------- | ------------ |
| You cannot install the app in HubSpot | Your HubSpot user lacks the **App Marketplace** permission. Ask a Super Admin. |
| The agent says it cannot see a record | Your HubSpot user has no access to it. The agent follows your HubSpot permissions. |
| A write action is refused | Your user has no write access to that object, or your subscription does not include it. Ask the agent to run `get_user_details`. |
| A new feature asks for more access | HubSpot added tools that need new scopes. Disconnect, then connect again. |
| The connection stops working | The access was revoked in HubSpot, or the integration is turned off. Check **Enabled**, click **Test connection**, or connect again. |

Need help? Contact [hello@cominty.ai](mailto:hello@cominty.ai).
